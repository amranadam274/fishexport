ZAB ENTERPRISE is based in Santoshpur, Kolkata, India. We specialise in dried fish maws, fish scale, fish by-products, and chilled and frozen fishes.

Our aim is to build long-term business relationships through product-focused service, clear communication and dependable coordination. We welcome enquiries from wholesalers, traders, distributors and potential business partners.
